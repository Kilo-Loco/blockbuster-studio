This guide takes you from nothing to your first image. You'll need a [Runpod](https://www.runpod.io) account with some credit on it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the deploy">
    Go to [blockbuster.studio](https://blockbuster.studio) and click **Deploy on Runpod**. There's one studio with everything in it: films with consistent characters, props and locations, video with sound, character voices, and Perform. Its models (about 128 GB) download the first time it starts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deploy the pod">
    On Runpod's deploy page, **click the RTX PRO 4500** GPU card. It's the card the studio is tested on. If it isn't available, pick another card with 32 GB or more, like an RTX 5090.

    Next, **click Add volume** and keep the suggested size (180 GB). This is where your models and films live. Don't skip it: without a volume the models don't fit, and your work is lost when the pod stops.

    You don't need to change anything under **Set overrides**. `STUDIO_PASSWORD` shows `change-me`, which the studio ignores: you'll create your password in the studio, and API keys go on its Settings page. (If you want a smaller install, you can switch model downloads off here, under **Set overrides**; see [Settings](/manage/settings) for the full list.)

    Then click **Deploy Pod**.

    :::warning
    Without a volume, your models and projects have nowhere to live. If you forget it, the studio shows a banner asking you to redeploy with one.
    :::
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open your studio">
    When the pod is running, click **Connect**, then **HTTP Service**. The studio opens in a new tab.

    Your studio's address looks like `https://<POD_ID>-3000.proxy.runpod.net`. Bookmark it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your password">
    On the first screen, choose a password of at least 8 characters and click **Create password & enter**.

    Your studio is on the public internet, and this password is the only thing that keeps others out. Let your browser save it.

    :::note
    For safety, a new studio can only be claimed in the **first 15 minutes** after the pod starts. If you miss the window, restart the pod from Runpod to reopen setup.
    :::
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the models">
    The first boot downloads the models in the background. An amber banner at the top of the studio shows each download's progress.

    Features unlock as their models arrive. Everything is usually ready in about **10 minutes**, depending on the machine's network. Until a feature's models are ready, its **Generate** button is disabled. Hover over it to see *Model files still downloading*.

    This only happens once. The models stay on your volume when you stop and restart the pod.

    :::note
    The video models need a free **Hugging Face token** before they can download. Make a free account at [huggingface.co](https://huggingface.co), open the [LTX-2.5](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5) and [LTX-2.5 Ingredients](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5-22b-IC-LoRA-Ingredients) pages and accept their terms, then [create a token](https://huggingface.co/settings/tokens) and paste it into the field under the waiting download (or on the [Settings](/manage/settings) page). The download starts within about 15 seconds.
    :::
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make your first image">
    On the **Create** page, make sure **Image** is selected at the bottom, describe a shot, and press **Generate** (or <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd>). Your image appears in the gallery a few seconds later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

:::tip[Save money]
You pay for the GPU the whole time the pod is running, even when you're not generating. When you're done for the day, [stop the pod](/manage/stop-and-back-up). Don't terminate it: terminating deletes everything.
:::
