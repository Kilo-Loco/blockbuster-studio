---
title: The recommended workflow
description: The order of steps that gives the most consistent films, done by hand or by an AI agent.
sidebar:
  icon: map
---

You can do every step yourself, or ask a connected [AI agent](/manage/settings#agent-access) to do it for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cast the scene">
    Give every person a face close-up and a turnaround, give key props (a car, a bag) a turnaround, and give the location an establishing shot.

    - **By hand:** in [Characters and locations](/guides/characters-and-locations).
    - **Agent:** *"Cast Rosa, her yellow convertible, and a coast road."*
  </Step>

  <Step title="Block out the scene">
    Make a plain grey 3D blockout (a *previs*) that sets the camera moves and cuts, and add it to the scene.

    - **By hand:** build it in Blender, then click **Previs** on the scene and upload it. See [Make a film with a previs](/guides/previs).
    - **Agent:** *"Previs scene 1 in Blender and add it to the scene."* The agent needs Blender on the computer it runs on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the reference sheet">
    One image with the scene's cast, props and location. It keeps them looking the same in every shot.

    - **By hand:** click **Build sheet** on the scene's **Reference sheet** card.
    - **Agent:** *"Build the reference sheet for scene 1."*
  </Step>

  <Step title="Try it fast (optional)">
    Render the shots at **Fast** quality (about 480p) to check the timing and camera moves quickly.

    - **By hand:** set **Quality** to **Fast** and click **Animate**.
    - **Agent:** *"Render scene 1 at 480p so I can check the timing."*
  </Step>

  <Step title="Render the final shots in HD">
    Render again at **HD** and redo any shot you don't like.

    - **By hand:** set **Quality** to **HD** and click **Animate**. If a shot skips a cut, raise **Follow previs**.
    - **Agent:** *"Render the final shots in HD and redo any that don't match the sheet."*
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export">
    Join the shots into one film. Add **Film look** if you want it, and **4K** for the final version (slow, so do it last).

    - **By hand:** on the **Timeline** tab, click **Export film**.
    - **Agent:** *"Export the film with the film look, in 4K."*
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also give an agent the whole job at once: *"Make a 10-second film of Rosa driving the coast road, using the recommended workflow."*
